Hi, welcome to my homepage !

I'm a software and applied machine learning engineer, finishing my master degree in software engineering at HES-SO (planned for Feb 2027) that I started to specialize my skills after a few years in the public sector.

I've worked as a software engineer (Feb 2022–Aug 2026) at EPFL+ECAL Lab, a design research and innovation center where I developed machine learning-driven interactive systems for design research projects, collaborating with a transdisciplinary team of designers, engineers and psychologists.

Some projects I worked on:

  • Èvokâ II: Permanent installation that links personal memories with regional media archives to explore local history and shared identity.
  • SWITCH Miso: Conversational agent that builds healthy and sustainable eating habits through structured microlearning, daily tips, and guided reflection.
  • Praeludium: Immersive research installation that combines spatialized 3D audio, dynamic colors, and geometric patterns controlled in real time by a musical mediator to enhance the audience's perception and emotional connection with classical music.
  • Open Books: Interactive installation that uses artificial intelligence to help visitors explore, compare, and perceive the hidden visual and editorial structures of photography books.
  • Things that Talk: Interactive exhibition project that uses custom physical controllers and digital screens to let visitors uncover interlocking historical, political, and social narratives centered around a single scientific instrument.

Before that, I worked as a research assistant (Sep 2018–Jan 2022) at the Institute ICT from HEIG‑VD on natural language processing applications such as a conversational question answering agent, an interactive French poem generator and a low-resource machine translation system.
